Welcome to Varanasi

The City of Light & Spiritual Awakening

Varanasi, originally called Kashi, derives from the word kasha meaning bright — the city of light. Banaras, as it is popularly known, sits on the banks of the sacred Ganga, where pilgrims have sought enlightenment for millennia.
